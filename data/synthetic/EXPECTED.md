# Expected results for the seeded scenarios

Generated by `python -m app.documents.make_synthetic` from the SCENARIOS table. `tests/test_demo.py` fails if the pipeline disagrees with this file.

Default for every scenario: claims on issuer-signed documents are **Verified**; claims on the application form and financial statement are **Insufficient evidence** (self-declared / attestation check not built in this version). Only the differences are listed.

| Scenario | What was seeded | Expected differences |
|---|---|---|
| `clean` | Everything consistent, every proof valid and recorded. | none (the baseline) |
| `forged_qr` | Land deed QR is signed by an untrusted key and points to a look-alike domain. | `land_deed.*` -> Contradicted |
| `extended_validity` | Printed 'valid until' is 30 June 2029, but the issuer signed 30 June 2027. | `fire_noc.*` -> Contradicted |
| `expired_fire_noc` | Genuine fire NOC whose validity ended 31 March 2026. | `fire_noc.*` -> Expired |
| `intake_mismatch` | Documents state an intake of 120; the mock AICTE record approves 60. | `*.approved_intake` -> Contradicted |
| `survey_mismatch` | Land use certificate cites survey 46/1; every other document says 45/2. | `*.survey_no` -> Contradicted |
| `missing_doc` | A required document is absent although later chain documents depend on it. | `land_use_certificate` -> Missing (listed as missing document) |

`*` means every field of that document type, or that field in every document.

## Run-time options (not baked into the PDFs)

- `outage=fire_department` on `clean`: fire NOC claims -> **Source unavailable**.
- `aicte_outage=true`: intake is not compared with the regulator; a note appears under case issues.

## Known limits

- Proof binding covers the fields in each document type's `bind_fields`. A printed field outside that list (for example `permitted_use`) is not cross-checked against the signature.
- Scans are read with English-only OCR and a page quality gate. Pages that fail it give **Blurry scan**, and a scan whose identifying fields were read with low confidence is never called Contradicted. Tested on rasterised copies of these PDFs (`tests/test_scan.py`), not on real scans.
- Text PDFs: the QR link is read from the printed text, falling back to a QR image. Scans: the QR image only.
- Rules are illustrative until checked against the official Handbook.
